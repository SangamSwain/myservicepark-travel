About Chilika Lagoon

Spanning over 1,100 square kilometers, Chilika Lake is Asia's largest brackish water lagoon. During peak winter, over 160 species of migratory birds fly in from Siberia, Central Asia, and the Caspian Sea.

Key Places to Visit

  • Satapada: Best spot for boat safaris to catch glimpses of rare Irrawaddy Dolphins.
  • Mangalajodi: Quiet marshland village famous for silent hand-paddled boat rides right next to bird flocks.
  • Kalijai Temple: Island shrine surrounded by blue lagoon waters.

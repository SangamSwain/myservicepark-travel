About Chilika Lake

Spanning over 1,100 square kilometers, Chilika Lake is Asia's largest brackish water lagoon. The wetland ecosystem hosts over 160 species of birds during peak winter, including migrants from Siberia, Mongolia, and Central Asia.

Top Experiences

  • Satapada Dolphin Sanctuary: Ideal for boat safaris to spot endangered Irrawaddy Dolphins.
  • Mangalajodi Marshlands: World-famous community-managed bird habitat offering non-motorized silent boat tours.
  • Kalijai Island Temple: Historic island shrine situated in the center of the lagoon.

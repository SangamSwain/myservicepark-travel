About Chilika Lake

Spanning over 1,100 square kilometers during the monsoons, Chilika Lake is Asia's largest brackish water lagoon and a UNESCO World Heritage tentative site. The ecosystem hosts over 160 species of birds during peak winter, including long-distance migrants from Siberia, Mongolia, and the Caspian Sea.

Key Highlights & Experiences

  • Satapada Dolphin Sanctuary: Located on the eastern side of the lagoon, Satapada is famous for boat safaris to spot rare, endangered Irrawaddy Dolphins in open waters.
  • Mangalajodi Bird Village: A community-managed marshland area on the northern edge of Chilika, world-famous for non-motorized silent boat rides that allow close observation of thousands of migratory birds.
  • Kalisai & Rajhans Island: Scenic natural sandbar strips where the lagoon meets the Bay of Bengal ocean.
  • Kalijai Temple: A revered island shrine dedicated to Goddess Kalijai, situated right in the middle of the lake.

How to Reach

Depending on which part of Chilika you are visiting, transportation routes differ:

  • For Satapada (Dolphin Spotting): 50 km drive from Puri town (approx. 1.5 hours by road).
  • For Mangalajodi (Bird Watching): 70 km drive from Bhubaneswar via NH-16 (approx. 2 hours).
  • By Train: Balugaon is the nearest major railway station connecting central parts of Chilika.

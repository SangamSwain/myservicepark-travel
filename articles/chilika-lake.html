Asia’s Largest Brackish Water Lagoon

Spread over 1,100 square kilometers, Chilika Lake is an ecological masterpiece. Home to rare Irrawaddy dolphins, millions of migratory birds from Siberia and Central Asia, and tranquil fishing villages, it remains one of Eastern India’s top natural destinations.

Top Experiences in Chilika

  • Mangalajodi Bird Sanctuary: Take a silent hand-rowed wooden boat to witness godwits, sandpipers, and flamingos up close.
  • Satapada Dolphin Cruises: Rent an OTDC motorboat to spot playful Irrawaddy dolphins in open water.
  • Kalijai Temple Island: Take a ferry trip across the deep bay to visit the island shrine revered by local fishermen.

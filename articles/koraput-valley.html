Koraput Valley: Misty Mountains & Organic Coffee Estates

By MyServicePark Editorial • Updated October 2026 • 5 Min Read

The Jewel of Southern Odisha

Nestled high in the Eastern Ghats at an elevation of 3,000 feet, Koraput features rolling green hills, dense pine forests, cascading waterfalls, and lush organic Arabica coffee plantations. It offers a cool climate retreat away from urban noise.

1 / 2
📍 Koraput Coffee Estates
Shade-grown organic coffee plantations nestled under mountain canopies.

Must-See Attractions

  • Duduma Waterfall: A spectacular 175-meter drop located along the border of Odisha and Andhra Pradesh.
  • Deomali Peak: The highest mountain peak in Odisha, offering camping sites and panoramic cloud views.
  • Koraput Tribal Museum & Coffee Tours: Learn about rich tribal traditions and sample freshly brewed Koraput Arabica coffee.

About Gopalpur Beach

Gopalpur, historically known as Gopalpur-on-Sea, is a tranquil coastal town situated on the Bay of Bengal coast in the Ganjam district of Odisha. Once a thriving seaport during the British maritime era, Gopalpur has transformed into one of Eastern India's most peaceful beach escapes.

Key Attractions & Things to Do

  • The Old Lighthouse: Offers panoramic 360-degree views of the coastline and palm groves. (Open in the evenings).
  • Sunrise Walk: Experience pristine, uncrowded morning walks along golden sands.
  • Water Sports & Beach Promenade: Enjoy local beach shacks, coconut water, and gentle water activities.
  • Colonial Port Ruins: Explore old dilapidated brick structures that remind visitors of its ancient trading past.

How to Reach

Gopalpur is exceptionally easy to reach via Berhampur:

  • By Train: Book a train to Brahmapur (BAM). Auto-rickshaws, buses, and private taxis run frequently to the beach (25-30 min ride).
  • By Road: Connected via National Highway 16. It is approximately a 3.5 to 4-hour drive from Bhubaneswar.

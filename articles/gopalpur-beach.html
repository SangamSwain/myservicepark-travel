Ganjam Coastal Retreat

Gopalpur-on-Sea: Colonial Charm & Golden Shorelines

By MyServicePark Editorial • Updated October 2026 • 4 Min Read

A Quiet Haven on the Bay of Bengal

Located in Ganjam district, Gopalpur-on-Sea is a tranquil coastal town renowned for its clean golden sands, historic red-and-white striped lighthouse, and relaxed pace of life. Once an active trading port during the British era, it now serves as a peaceful getaway for beachgoers.

Highlights & Things to Do

  • Gopalpur Lighthouse Visit: Climb the historic tower during afternoon visiting hours for sweeping views of the sea and town.
  • Seafood & Promenade Walks: Enjoy fresh grilled coastal seafood at beachside stalls while strolling along the paved beach promenade.
  • Excursion to Tampara Lake: Located nearby, Tampara Lake offers thrilling water sports like jet skiing and kayaking.

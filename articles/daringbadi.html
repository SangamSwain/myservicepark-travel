Daringbadi: The Kashmir of Odisha

By MyServicePark Editorial • Updated October 2026 • 5 Min Read

Pine Forests & Misty Highlands

Situated at an elevation of 3,000 feet in Kandhamal district, Daringbadi is popular as the "Kashmir of Odisha". Surrounded by thick pine forests, cascading waterfalls, and sprawling organic coffee plantations, it is one of the few places in Odisha where winter temperatures occasionally drop near freezing point.

Top Experiences in Daringbadi

  • Pine Jungle & Nature Park: Walk through serene pine groves and explore the landscaped hilltop gardens.
  • Coffee & Black Pepper Plantations: Visit state-maintained coffee estates and learn about local highland farming.
  • Midubanda & Dasingbadi Waterfalls: Scenic forest waterfalls located within a short drive from the main town.
  • Belghar Sanctuary Excursion: A wilderness reserve famous for wild elephants, wooden watchtowers, and indigenous Kutia Kondh tribal culture.

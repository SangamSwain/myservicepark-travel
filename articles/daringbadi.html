About Daringbadi

Perched at an elevation of 3,000 feet above sea level in the Kandhamal district, Daringbadi is Odisha’s premier hill station. Named after Daring Sahib, a British officer who oversaw the region during colonial rule, the town is famously known as the "Kashmir of Odisha" due to its dense pine forests, rolling coffee plantations, cascading waterfalls, and crisp, chilly winter climate where temperature occasionally drops near freezing point.

Top Places to See in Daringbadi

  • Coffee & Pepper Plantations: Spread across vast acres, these quiet gardens are maintained by the forest department and offer a refreshing morning walk.
  • Pine Forest & Lover's Point: Soaring pine trees and scenic valleys ideal for photography and nature walks.
  • Midubanda & Dasingbadi Waterfalls: Beautiful natural cascades surrounded by lush greenery, perfect for day picnics.
  • Hill View Point: A dedicated watchtower area offering uninterrupted vistas of the entire valley during sunset.
  • Emu Farm: A unique sanctuary where visitors can observe large Australian Emus kept in a scenic natural enclosure.

How to Reach Daringbadi

Reaching Daringbadi requires a road trip through winding ghat roads lined with teak and sal trees:

  • By Road: Daringbadi is well connected by Odisha State Road Transport Corporation (OSRTC) buses and private taxis from Bhubaneswar (approx. 6 hours) and Berhampur (approx. 3.5 hours).
  • By Train: The nearest major railhead is Brahmapur (BAM). Taxis and buses are readily available outside the station.

Frequently Asked Questions

Daringbadi does not experience heavy snowfall. However, during peak winter nights (December to January), temperatures can drop to 0°C or sub-zero levels, causing thick frost to form over grass and foliage that resembles a thin layer of snow in early mornings.

A 2-day, 1-night itinerary is ideal to cover all major spots like the coffee gardens, waterfalls, pine trees, and hill viewpoints comfortably.
